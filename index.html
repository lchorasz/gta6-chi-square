GTA 6 vs. GPA 🎮

Release-week scenario • November 16–20, 2026

With Grand Theft Auto VI arriving on Thursday, November 19, 2026, college administrators wonder whether students' absences follow their usual equal weekday pattern—or whether release day and the day after look different. Could Vice City be calling louder than class?

Statistical question: Does the distribution of absences across weekdays differ from the college's historically equal weekday pattern? All numbers below are fictional classroom data.

3. Ask the class

How high must Thursday's absences climb before the result becomes statistically significant? What changes if Friday's absences also increase?
Big question: If we reject H₀, have we proved that GTA 6 caused students to skip class? No! The test detects a change in the distribution, not its cause.

Teaching note: A goodness-of-fit test compares weekday proportions, not the overall absence rate. Real analyses would need reliable historical benchmarks and comparable sampling conditions. For a chi-square approximation, expected counts should generally be at least 5; all slider settings here satisfy that condition. This page works offline and uses no external libraries.
